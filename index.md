# Privacy Policy for DNEWS

**Last Updated:** November 20, 2026

DNEWS ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DNEWS Android TV and Google TV application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DNEWS application.

## 1. Information We Collect
DNEWS is designed to work without account registration or login. We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, home address, phone number, or payment details.

App preferences such as your selected language (among the 12 supported languages), category selections, or display settings may be stored locally on your device to provide the requested functionality.

## 2. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising
DNEWS integrates third-party services—including **Google Play Services** and **Google AdMob**—to deliver advertisements, maintain app stability, and measure performance.

Depending on your device settings, region, and Google account preferences, these third-party services may automatically process certain information such as:
* Advertising ID (AAID) and device/app identifiers
* Diagnostic data, crash logs, and performance metrics
* General non-precise location information (e.g., country or region derived from IP/network)

Data processed by third-party SDKs is governed by their respective privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID in your device/TV settings where supported.

## 3. News Content and External Links
News headlines, summaries, and related items shown in DNEWS are aggregated from public RSS and news feeds. All trademarks, logos, and copyrights belong to their respective publishers and content owners.

If you open external content or publisher links, those third-party websites or services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. Data Retention and Security
Because DNEWS does not operate custom user accounts or maintain a personal user profile database of its own, we do not store personal account records on our own servers. Reasonable technical measures are taken in line with platform standards to keep the application secure.

## 5. Children's Privacy
DNEWS is not directed specifically at children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing without parental involvement, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, SDK changes, or legal/store requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
